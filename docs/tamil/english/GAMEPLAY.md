# Gameplay Requirements

## Cards and teams

- Tables: 4 or 6 players; 6 cards per player.
- Alternating teams: 4 seats are P1/P3 vs P2/P4; 6 seats are P1/P3/P5 vs P2/P4/P6.
- Ranks: 2, 3, J, 9, A, 10; six-player tables also use K, Q, 8.
- Strength: 2, 3, J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8.
- Points: 100, 50, 30, 20, 11, 10, 3, 2, 0 respectively.

## Dealing and bidding

- Local dealing is animated as two passes of 3 cards each.
- Bidding is one lap, minimum 500, steps of 10, maximum claim 903.
- 4 passes or 6 passes causes a redeal for the current table size, preserving names
  and match-level penalty cards.

## Trump and play

- The claimer selects one real card in two clicks: open hand, click card.
- The card leaves the hand face down, is unplayable, and does not satisfy follow suit.
- The card returns when trump is asked; if nobody asks, it returns for the final trick
  as an ordinary card.
- The player immediately before the claimer leads; the claimer plays second.
- Trick winners lead subsequent tricks.
- Follow suit when possible. A void player may play any legal card and may optionally ask.
- Hidden trump does not win; revealed trump beats non-trump.

## Double and surrender

- Double is claimer-only, confirmed with the final card, and appears only after the
  claimer team wins the first five tricks. Face-down trump blocks Double.
- During play, if the opposing team has no trump cards remaining while at least one
  trump remains elsewhere, the opposing team surrenders. If all trump cards are gone,
  this rule does not trigger. Surrender ends the round with no score or penalty count.

## Settlement

- Made claim: opponents pay 1 card under 700, 3 at 700+.
- Failed claim: claiming team pays 2 under 700, 3 at 700+.
- Ordinary failed shortfall below half adds 1.
- Correct Double adds 1; wrong Double replaces settlement with 3 or 4 cards.
